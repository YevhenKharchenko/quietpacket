How to Play

Match, combine, progress

  • Match Tiles

    Swap pieces to align three or more identical tiles.

  • Create Combos

    Form larger matches to trigger powerful effects.

  • Complete Objectives

    Focus on level goals to move forward.

  • Use Boosters

    Activate special tools for tricky situations.

  • Unlock New Levels

    Advance through increasingly varied challenges.
